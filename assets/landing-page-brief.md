# RiseV — Landing Page Content Brief

## 🧠 What is RiseV? (Hero Headline Context)

RiseV is a **physical-to-digital loyalty ecosystem** for local businesses.

**Core product**: A physical NFC stand placed on the merchant's counter, bundled with a cloud software platform that handles digital loyalty cards, WhatsApp automation, CRM, and analytics.

**The core problem we solve:**
> Local businesses (cafes, barbers, car washes, bubble tea, etc.) lose customers because:
> - Paper stamp cards get lost
> - No way to follow up after a customer visits
> - Zero data about who their customers are

**Our one-liner:**
> "Know who your customers are. Bring them back automatically. Reward their loyalty — without them ever downloading an app."

## 👤 Customer Journey (for How It Works section)

1. **Customer walks in** → taps phone on the NFC stand at the counter
2. **Phone opens instantly** → no app download, no QR scan
3. **Quick registration** → enters name and phone number (30 seconds)
4. **Loyalty card created** → stamp card saved on their phone
5. **WhatsApp received** → "Welcome to [Store]! You have 1 stamp."
6. **Next visit** → merchant adds stamps (via NFC tap, QR, or manual)
7. **WhatsApp reminder** → "You're 2 stamps away from your reward!"
8. **Stamps complete** → voucher unlocked automatically
9. **Voucher redeemed** → staff scans QR code, reward given
10. **Birthday** → auto WhatsApp birthday voucher sent

## ❓ FAQ Content

**Q: Does the customer need to download an app?**
A: No. When the customer taps the NFC stand, the loyalty card opens directly in their phone's browser. No app download required.

**Q: Does it work on iPhone and Android?**
A: Yes. The NFC stand works with both iOS and Android phones out of the box.

**Q: How does the WhatsApp automation work?**
A: RiseV connects to the WhatsApp Cloud API. Messages are sent automatically based on your customer's activity — stamps earned, vouchers unlocked, birthdays, and win-back triggers.

**Q: How long does setup take?**
A: Under 10 minutes. Set up your store profile, configure your stamp card design and reward, place the stand — and you're live.

**Q: Can I see who my customers are?**
A: Yes. Every customer who taps your stand is added to your CRM with their name, phone number, birthday, and full transaction history.

**Q: Can I have multiple staff members?**
A: Yes. Pro and Enterprise plans support multiple staff accounts for stamp issuance and voucher redemption.

**Q: What happens when a customer completes their stamp card?**
A: A voucher is automatically generated and the customer receives a WhatsApp message with their unique redemption code. Staff scan the code to mark it as used.

**Q: Can I customize the loyalty card design?**
A: Yes, fully. You can set the card background color, stamp icon, font color, and upload a custom background image — so the card reflects your brand.
